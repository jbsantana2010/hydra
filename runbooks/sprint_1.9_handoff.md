Date: 2026-05-10  
Executor: Codex

## Implementation Summary

Sprint 1.9 improves the commercial presentation quality of generated product packages. The package builder now produces preview assets, presentation briefs, a simple PDF export, and a richer manifest so the generated ZIP feels more upload-ready for Gumroad/Etsy/Fiverr-style workflows.

No marketplace APIs, publishing automation, image generation, scraping, scheduler, Celery, or browser automation was added.

## Known Issues

- PDF export is intentionally simple and text-based. It is enough for a local sellable-package scaffold, but styled PDF rendering remains a future quality upgrade.
- Preview assets are HTML specs/previews, not generated image files. This sprint deliberately avoids image generation and browser automation.
- Files under `products/` are generated by the container and may be owned by the container user.
